Difference between ArrayList and LinkedList:
~ArrayList is best Retrieval and LinkedList is best choice in the insertion and deletion in the middle.
~ArrayList is the worst choice if our frequent operation is insertion or deletion and LinkedList is the worst choice if our frequent operation is retrieval operation.
~underlaying data structures for arrayList is resizable or growable array and underlying data structure is double linked List.
~ArrayList implements random-access interface and LinkedList doesn't implements random-access Interface.
